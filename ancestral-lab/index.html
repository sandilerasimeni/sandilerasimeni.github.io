02

What the model does—and doesn’t do

Transparent about the boundary between machine learning and imagination.

A

CNN face detection

TensorFlow.js BlazeFace, a pre-trained convolutional neural network, locates a face and facial landmarks in your browser. Those coordinates only initialize simple geometric illustration parameters.

B

Stochastic trait simulation

A seeded random process mutates a small, illustrative parameter vector across the selected number of generations. The SVG portraits are drawn by this app; they are not generated by a trained face decoder.

C

Why no VAE yet?

A useful variational autoencoder needs a suitable, licensed face dataset and training. One uploaded image is not enough to train a meaningful VAE or infer inheritance. This demo does not pretend otherwise.
